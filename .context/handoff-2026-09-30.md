## Facts worth remembering

- **Merging is the deploy** for the Worker (any push to `main`) and for the S3 collectors on nemaring, which reset to `origin/main` before each run.
  The Umami pusher is the exception: it does not self-update, so a merge to nemar-umami `main` needs `git -C /opt/nemar-umami pull --ff-only` on nemaring.
  Only a change under `ops/systemd/` needs `sudo /opt/nemar-observability/ops/install-units.sh`.
- **Snapshot cron is at :47** because nemar-cli's hourly `nemar-db-backup` export locks D1 between about :10 and :40; `CRON_RETRY` waits out a locked read for about 5 minutes.
- **Health rules:** a series must hold the newest closed UTC day six hours after midnight; a first-party section needs a successful run within 26 hours (`ingested_sections.last_ok_at`); Umami must be configured, reachable, and have seen an event in six hours.
  The website pusher signals failure by not posting, and its `collector_health` warning is a display level, not a failure.
- **Umami `unit=day` limit** follows a calendar-month rule (`differenceInCalendarMonths <= 7`), read from upstream 3.4.0 and consistent with measurements of 241 days daily and 242 monthly for one end date.
  It was not verified beyond those points and a 4, 7 and 8 month probe.
  The README in nemar-umami has a read-only probe to re-check it after an Umami upgrade.
- **Verifying a deploy:** cache-bust the snapshot (`?cb=$(date +%s)`); `/health` is `no-store`.
  A post-deploy check can fail only on Worker-owned facts (see `deploy.yml`); the PR #87 deploy showed red because of the D1 lock, not the build.
- **Health alert titles** are capped at 250 characters (GitHub rejects over 256; the limit was not tested against the live API).
